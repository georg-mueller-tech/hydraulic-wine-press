Project 01 · Apprenticeship final project

Hydraulic wine press

Journeyman's piece, Austrian apprenticeship exam in mechanical engineering Werkschulheim Felbertal, Salzburg Sep 2021 to Jul 2022

For the final exam of my apprenticeship I designed and built a hand-operated hydraulic press for grapes and fruit mash. I worked on it alone for ten months. I made the SolidWorks model and the full drawing set, wrote the CNC programs in CAMWorks and did the machining, welding, painting and assembly in the school workshop.

Basket, collecting tray and the hydraulics (hand pump, cylinder, hose) are bought parts. Frame, press head, cross grid, pins and plates are my own design, and I made them myself.

I did the apprenticeship alongside the Austrian upper secondary school diploma (Matura) at Werkschulheim Felbertal. The final exam is built around a machine each apprentice designs and makes on their own.

Design decisions

AForce path

The cylinder sits in the press head and pushes down on the mash. The reaction goes through the lower press plate into a welded cross grid, and two Ø40 mm pins carry it into the columns (80 × 80 mm hollow sections). A spacer rod between the columns keeps the frame from racking.

BAdjustable height

The cross grid can be pinned in three positions, 150 mm apart. Together with the 400 mm cylinder stroke this works for a full 18 L basket and for small batches.

CFood contact

Parts that touch the mash are unpainted and come off for cleaning. The basket is stainless steel (X2CrNi19-11). The plate on the cylinder has a raised rim, so oil from a leaking seal or hose does not run straight into the juice.

DSecond use

With basket and tray removed it is a 2.5 t workshop press, for example for pressing bearings in or out. Welded feet with side plates keep the 2 m frame from tipping.

Documentation

Besides the drawings I wrote a 35-page project folder with operating manual, cleaning and maintenance instructions, a troubleshooting guide, the torque table and a full cost calculation. Work breakdown, milestones, resource plan and Gantt chart are in a separate project handbook. The material budget was about €1,000.
